# Privacy Policy for Inside Nepal Automation

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Inside Nepal Automation is used to help manage and publish content to Facebook Pages administered by the app owner.

## Information we access
The app may use Facebook permissions that allow the authenticated Page administrator to manage Pages and publish Page content. We do not sell personal information.

## How information is used
Information obtained through Meta/Facebook is used only to authenticate the authorized Page administrator and to perform Page-management functions requested by the administrator, such as publishing posts, photos, and videos.

## Data sharing
We do not sell or rent user data. We do not share Facebook user data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

## Data retention
We retain only the minimum information needed to operate the automation. Access tokens and credentials are stored securely in private automation secrets and are not published in this repository.

## Data deletion
If you want data associated with this app deleted, remove the app from your Facebook/Meta account permissions and revoke its access. You may also contact the app owner through the Facebook Page associated with the app to request deletion of any retained app data.

## Security
Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and automation data from unauthorized access.

## Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated when the app or Meta platform requirements change.

## Contact
For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the administrator through the Inside Nepal Facebook Page.
