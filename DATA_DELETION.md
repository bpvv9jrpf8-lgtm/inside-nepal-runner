# Inside Nepal Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Inside Nepal is an administrator-only Facebook Page automation. It does not provide public user accounts or maintain a separate consumer profile database.

If you have authorized the app and want app-related data associated with your use removed, revoke the app from your Facebook/Meta account settings and send a deletion request to the contact email listed in the Meta app settings for Inside Nepal.

Please include enough information to identify the authorization you want removed. We will delete or disconnect app-held authorization-related data that we control, subject to any legal or security retention requirements.

Revoking the app in Meta also prevents future access using that authorization.
